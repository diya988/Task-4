# WeatherNow – Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 14 November 2026

## 1. Introduction
WeatherNow is a weather application that allows users to search for cities and view current weather conditions and forecasts.

We respect your privacy and aim to protect the information collected while using the application.

## 2. Information We Collect
- **Account information:** Email address and authentication information when you register or log in using Firebase Authentication.
- **City searches:** City names entered manually by users to retrieve weather information.
- **App preferences:** Favourite cities and other preferences stored locally on your device.

WeatherNow does not request access to your device's GPS location.

## 3. Third-Party Services

WeatherNow uses:

- **Firebase Authentication (Google):** For user registration and login.
- **Open-Meteo:** For city geocoding and weather forecast information.

These services may process information according to their respective privacy policies.

## 4. Data Storage
Account authentication is handled by Firebase. Favourite cities and app preferences are stored locally on your device.

## 5. Data Sharing
WeatherNow does not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by the third-party services mentioned above to provide app functionality.

## 6. Children's Privacy
WeatherNow is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

## 7. Security
We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the application. However, no electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure.

## 8. Changes to This Policy
This Privacy Policy may be updated when application features or data practices change.
